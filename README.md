# Hi there, I'm Katie Millar 👋

I'm a **PhD Student at the University of East Anglia (UEA)**, researching **host–microbe symbioses in the Mediterranean fruit fly (*Ceratitis capitata*)**. 🪰🦠

My research combines **molecular biology, microbiology, ecology, evoloution and bioinformatics** to investigate interactions between fruit flies and their microbiota.

## I'm currently working on

- 🪰 **Medfly (*Ceratitis capitata*) microbiota and host–microbe interactions**
- Shotgun metagenomics and microbial community profiling
- Microbial transmission and host–microbe symbioses
- Experimental approaches to investigate the role of microbiota in Medfly biology

##  I'm currently learning

- Microbial genome and functional annotation
